# OffMap brand and visual language

`hero_section_gm.png` is the approved reference for energy and composition, not a flattened screen to reproduce.

## Voice

Optimistic, direct, curious, and student-to-student. Prefer “Find what could change your next step” over fantasy, corporate, or prestige language. Never use “Enchanted Map”, quests, unicorn imagery, or claims that OffMap runs or endorses listed opportunities.

## Visual system

- Warm paper ground `#F5EEDC` with near-black ink `#111111`.
- Electric blue `#1268FF`, orange `#FF5A24`, magenta `#F42AA4`, violet `#6E4BFF`, lime `#C9F43B`, and restrained teal `#0E9F8C`.
- `Archivo Black` for short display headlines, `DM Sans` for reading and UI, and `Caveat` for brief handwritten accents only.
- Torn-paper silhouettes, tape, dotted grids, route doodles, hand-drawn underlines, and imperfect rotations support hierarchy; they never reduce legibility.
- Use live, selectable text and responsive components. The reference image is reserved for documentation and social metadata.

## Accessibility

The initial release is an intentional light theme. All foreground/background pairs must meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support dynamic type, reflow, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, and at least 44 by 44 point native targets. Decorative doodles are hidden from accessibility trees.
