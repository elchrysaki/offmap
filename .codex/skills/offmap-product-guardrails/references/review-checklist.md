# Product review checklist

- OffMap is a directory, not an organizer, application tracker, social network, or endorsement system.
- Student accounts, profiles, documents, ratings, comments, communities, maps, stories, notifications, and dark mode are out of MVP scope.
- Saves remain local and contribution requires no identity.
- Detail prioritizes deadline, eligibility, funding/cost, location, verification, and official external action.
- `Archivo Black`, `DM Sans`, and brief `Caveat` accents use the shared design package.
- Decorative collage elements do not become unlabeled controls or obscure content.
- Minimum touch size is 44 by 44; keyboard focus is visible; motion respects reduced-motion settings.
